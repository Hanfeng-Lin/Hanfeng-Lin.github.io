**Abstract**

Central to developing targeted protein degradation (TPD) molecules such as PROTACs is the ability to measure degradation of a target protein accurately and sensitively. The NanoLuciferase (nLuc) and complementary split-nLuc systems are highly sensitive reporters for this purpose, but lysine residues on the tags can themselves be ubiquitinated, creating artifacts in which the tag rather than the target drives degradation.

This chapter details protocols for generating and using the lysine-free variants HiBiT-RR and nLuc<sup>K0</sup>, in which all lysine residues are replaced with arginine. These tools mitigate tag-driven degradation and improve the reliability of TPD screening assays. Methods are described for cellular degradation assays with both transiently expressed and endogenously tagged proteins, together with validation by immunoblotting.
