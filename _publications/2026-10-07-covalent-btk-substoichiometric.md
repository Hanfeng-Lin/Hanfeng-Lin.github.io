**Abstract**

The viability of covalent PROTACs has been debated, since irreversible binding appears to contradict the catalytic, sub-stoichiometric mechanism of action. This work develops PSIRC3, a potent covalent PROTAC for Bruton's tyrosine kinase (BTK), which induces selective degradation with a DC<sub>50</sub> of 0.75 nM and a D<sub>max</sub> of 85%, while its non-covalent counterpart is inactive. Activity requires covalent bond formation with Cys481 and is abolished against the C481S mutant. PSIRC3 reaches maximum degradation within 30 minutes, and a single in vivo administration produces substantial BTK degradation in both PBMCs (>80%) and splenocytes (>50%).
