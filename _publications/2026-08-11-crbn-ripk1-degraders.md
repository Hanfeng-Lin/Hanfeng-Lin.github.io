**Abstract**

RIPK1 regulates programmed cell death and mediates tumor resistance to immune checkpoint inhibitors. Through systematic optimization of the linker and the CRBN ligand portion, this work develops LD5095, a cereblon-recruiting RIPK1 degrader showing potent and selective degradation across cell lines with rapid kinetics and sustained effect over 72 h post-washout. RIPK1 degradation by LD5095 sensitized Jurkat cells to TNFα-induced apoptosis, and the compound exhibited metabolic stability and an extended half-life. A single in vivo dose achieved durable RIPK1 degradation in xenograft tumors over 6 days.
