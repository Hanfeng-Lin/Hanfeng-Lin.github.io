>
> 面向已经会做 FEP、第一次接触非平衡（NEQ）自由能方法的读者的双语讲解。每一节先给英文，再给中文，方便对照。

---

Non-equilibrium free energy simulation (NEQ) is a family of methods that estimate equilibrium free energy differences from the **work done during a non-equilibrium process**. In protein–ligand relative binding free energy (RBFE) calculations it is often compared with the traditional FEP/TI/BAR workflow, because both answer the same question: if we mutate ligand A into ligand B, how much does the binding free energy change?

非平衡自由能模拟（Non-equilibrium Free Energy Simulation, NEQ）是一类用"非平衡过程中的做功"来估计平衡态自由能差的方法。它在蛋白-配体相对结合自由能（RBFE）计算中，常被拿来和传统 FEP/TI/BAR 工作流比较，因为两者都在回答同一个问题：把配体 A 变成配体 B，结合自由能会改变多少？

If you already do FEP, the first time you hear about NEQ you will probably be a little confused: isn't free energy an equilibrium property? Why can a process that deliberately drives the system out of equilibrium and runs for only tens of picoseconds still recover the equilibrium $\Delta G$?

如果你已经会做 FEP，第一次听到 NEQ 时，大概率会有一点困惑：自由能不是平衡态性质吗？为什么一个故意把体系拉离平衡、只跑几十皮秒的过程，最后反而还能算出平衡态的 $\Delta G$？

That confusion is natural, because FEP trains an intuition that you must walk slowly through a series of $\lambda$ windows, sample each window as close to equilibrium as possible, and keep enough phase-space overlap between neighboring windows. Suddenly someone says, "you don't need all those intermediate windows — just sample the two end states well, then rapidly pull from A to B and back from B to A, and collect the work of these non-equilibrium trajectories." It sounds like it is destroying FEP's most precious assumption.

这个疑问很正常，因为 FEP 训练出来的直觉是：要在一串 $\lambda$ 窗口里慢慢走，每个窗口都要尽量采到平衡，窗口之间还要有足够的 phase space overlap。突然有人说"不用那么多中间窗口，先把两个端态采好，然后从 A 快速拉到 B、再从 B 快速拉回 A，统计这些非平衡轨迹的做功就行"，听起来像是在破坏 FEP 最珍贵的假设。

The key point of NEQ is not that it ignores the equilibrium state, but that it **reaches the same equilibrium free energy by a different route**. FEP asks: on a fixed configuration, if I instantly swap the Hamiltonian from $U_A$ to $U_B$, what is the energy difference? NEQ asks: if I actually drive the system from A to B over a finite time, how much work does the surroundings do on the system along the way? The Jarzynski equality and the Crooks fluctuation theorem tell us that as long as the starting points come from the correct equilibrium distribution, these non-equilibrium work values still contain the equilibrium $\Delta G$.

NEQ 的关键并不是无视平衡态，而是换了一种抵达平衡态自由能的路径。FEP 问的是：在同一个构象上，把哈密顿量从 $U_A$ 瞬间换成 $U_B$，能量差是多少？NEQ 问的是：如果我真的用一段有限时间把体系从 A 驱动到 B，这一路外界对体系做了多少功？Jarzynski 等式和 Crooks 涨落定理告诉我们，只要起点来自正确的平衡分布，这些非平衡做功里仍然包含着平衡态 $\Delta G$。

So this article starts from concepts familiar to FEP users and clarifies the points where people most often get stuck: why FEP can be called an "instantaneous jump," what the "short pull" in NEQ is actually pulling, why Crooks/BAR can extract the free energy from the forward and reverse work distributions, and — in terms of compute and accuracy — when NEQ is actually worth replacing or supplementing FEP.

所以这篇文章从 FEP 使用者熟悉的概念出发，把几个最容易卡住的点讲清楚：为什么 FEP 可以说是"瞬时跳跃"，NEQ 里的"短拉伸"到底拉的是什么，Crooks/BAR 为什么能从正反向做功分布里抽出自由能，以及在算力和准确性上，NEQ 到底什么时候值得替代或补充 FEP。

## The one-sentence version / 一句话版本

If you only remember one sentence, understand it like this:

如果只记住一句话，可以这样理解：

**FEP cuts the A→B change into many equilibrium windows and takes "frozen-coordinate" instantaneous energy differences between neighboring windows; NEQ only samples the two end states A and B thoroughly, then starts from end-state configurations and continuously changes the Hamiltonian over a finite time, recording the work of this non-equilibrium process.**

**FEP 把 A 到 B 的变化切成很多个平衡态窗口，在相邻窗口之间做"冻结坐标"的瞬时能量差；NEQ 只充分采样 A 和 B 两个端态，然后从端态构象出发，在有限时间内连续改变哈密顿量，并记录这个非平衡过程的做功。**

In other words, FEP slices in space; NEQ integrates in time.

也就是说，FEP 更像是在空间上切片，NEQ 更像是在时间上积分。

| Dimension / 维度 | FEP / BAR | NEQ / Crooks-BAR |
|---|---|---|
| Core object / 核心对象 | Potential energy difference $\Delta U$ between neighboring λ windows / 相邻 λ 窗口的势能差 $\Delta U$ | Non-equilibrium work $W$ for A→B and B→A / A 到 B、B 到 A 的非平衡做功 $W$ |
| Sampling focus / 采样重点 | Equilibrium sampling of many intermediate λ windows / 多个中间 λ 窗口的平衡采样 | Thorough sampling of the two physical end states / 两个物理端态的充分采样 |
| Compute structure / 计算结构 | Many windows, often with HREX / 多窗口、常配合 HREX | Many short, mutually independent trajectories / 大量短轨迹，彼此独立 |
| Main risk / 主要风险 | Under-sampled intermediate windows, poor phase-space overlap / 中间窗口采样不充分、相空间不重叠 | Excessive dissipated work, non-overlapping forward/reverse work distributions / 耗散功过大、正反向做功分布不重叠 |
| Better for / 更适合 | Standard small-molecule R-group tweaks, industrial SAR / 标准小分子 R 基团微调、产业化 SAR | Clear end-state conformations, high concurrency, notable local conformational rearrangement / 端态构象清楚、需要高并发、局部构象重排明显的体系 |

## Why FEP is called an "instantaneous jump" / FEP 为什么被说成"瞬时跳跃"

Many people find "FEP is an instantaneous jump" strange at first: standard FEP clearly sets up many λ windows, e.g. $\lambda=0,0.05,0.10,\dots,1.0$ — isn't that a gradual change?

很多人第一次听到"FEP 是瞬时跳跃"会觉得奇怪：标准 FEP 明明设置了很多个 λ 窗口，比如 $\lambda=0,0.05,0.10,\dots,1.0$，这不就是渐进变化吗？

Here we must distinguish the **macroscopic workflow** from the **minimal statistical unit**.

这里要区分**宏观工作流**和**最小统计单元**。

At the workflow level, FEP is indeed gradual. We deliberately insert many intermediate states so neighboring windows are similar enough, and run equilibrium MD sampling in each window. But when computing the free energy difference *between* neighboring windows, FEP uses the Zwanzig equation:

在宏观流程上，FEP 的确是渐进的。我们人为插入许多中间态，让相邻窗口足够相似，并在每个窗口里做平衡态 MD 采样。但在相邻窗口之间计算自由能差时，FEP 使用的是 Zwanzig 方程：

$$
e^{-\beta \Delta G_{i\to j}}
=
\left\langle e^{-\beta \left[U_j(r)-U_i(r)\right]} \right\rangle_i
$$

Here the average $\langle\cdot\rangle_i$ means configurations $r$ are drawn from the equilibrium ensemble of state $i$. The key point: for a specific configuration $r$, when we compute $U_j(r)-U_i(r)$, the coordinates do not move. The system has no time to relax and does not run any dynamics along the new potential energy surface. We simply take the same atomic coordinates and re-score them under a different Hamiltonian.

这里的平均 $\langle\cdot\rangle_i$ 表示构象 $r$ 来自状态 $i$ 的平衡系综。关键点在于：对某一个具体构象 $r$，我们计算 $U_j(r)-U_i(r)$ 时，坐标是不动的。系统没有时间弛豫，也没有沿着新势能面跑一小段动力学。我们只是把同一个原子坐标，放到另一个哈密顿量下面重新打分。

That is what "instantaneous jump" means. It does not mean the whole FEP workflow crudely jumps from $\lambda=0$ to $\lambda=1$; it means that each neighboring-window perturbation estimate is essentially a **frozen-coordinate vertical transition**.

这就是"瞬时跳跃"的含义。它不是说整个 FEP 流程粗暴地从 $\lambda=0$ 跳到 $\lambda=1$，而是说每一次相邻窗口的微扰估计，本质上都是一个冻结坐标下的垂直跃迁。

This also explains why FEP depends so heavily on phase-space overlap. If the configurations common in state $i$ are all high-energy configurations in state $j$, then the exponential average of $U_j-U_i$ is dominated by a few rare low-work events and convergence is very poor. So production-grade FEP usually needs:

这也解释了 FEP 为什么如此依赖相空间重叠。如果状态 $i$ 里常见的构象，在状态 $j$ 里都是高能构象，那么 $U_j-U_i$ 的指数平均会被极少数低功事件支配，收敛会非常差。所以生产级 FEP 往往需要：

- Dense enough λ windows; / 足够密的 λ 窗口；
- Soft-core potentials to avoid singularities when atoms are created/annihilated; / soft-core potentials 来避免原子生成/湮灭时的奇异性；
- Enhanced sampling such as HREX or REST; / HREX 或 REST 之类的增强采样；
- Closed-cycle analysis to detect inconsistencies in the thermodynamic network. / 闭环分析来识别热力学网络中的不一致。

## The theory behind NEQ: replacing energy differences with work / NEQ 的理论基础：把能量差换成做功

NEQ's starting point is not "instantly re-score between two neighboring equilibrium states," but "let the system genuinely undergo a time-dependent change process."

NEQ 的出发点不是"在两个相邻平衡态之间瞬时重打分"，而是"让系统真的经历一个含时变化过程"。

Suppose we start from an equilibrium configuration of state A and push $\lambda$ continuously from 0 to 1 over 50 ps. During the simulation the potential energy function changes and the atomic coordinates also evolve in time. This process is generally far from equilibrium, so each trajectory produces a non-equilibrium work $W$.

假设我们从状态 A 的一个平衡构象出发，在 50 ps 内把 $\lambda$ 从 0 连续推到 1。模拟过程中，势能函数在变，原子坐标也在随时间演化。这个过程通常远离平衡，因此每条轨迹都会产生一个非平衡做功 $W$。

The Jarzynski equality tells us that even though this process is non-equilibrium, as long as the initial configurations come from the equilibrium distribution of state A, the exponential average over many A→B trajectories still gives the equilibrium free energy difference:

Jarzynski 等式告诉我们，即使这个过程是非平衡的，只要初始构象来自状态 A 的平衡分布，很多条 A 到 B 的轨迹的指数平均仍然给出平衡态自由能差：

$$
e^{-\beta \Delta G}
=
\left\langle e^{-\beta W_{A\to B}} \right\rangle_A
$$

This formula is very elegant but hard to use directly, because the exponential average is dominated by rare low-work trajectories. If the pull is fast and the dissipated work is large, trajectories with $W<\Delta G$ are extremely rare, and the one-directional Jarzynski estimate becomes very unstable.

这条公式非常漂亮，但直接用它并不好算。因为指数平均会被罕见的低功轨迹支配。如果拉伸很快，耗散功很大，那些 $W<\Delta G$ 的轨迹极少出现，单向 Jarzynski 估计会非常不稳定。

The Crooks fluctuation theorem goes further and uses both the forward and reverse sets of trajectories:

Crooks 涨落定理进一步利用了正向和反向两组轨迹：

$$
\frac{P_F(W)}{P_R(-W)}
=
e^{\beta(W-\Delta G)}
$$

Here $P_F(W)$ is the A→B work distribution and $P_R(-W)$ is the mirrored B→A work distribution. One intuitive conclusion: when $W=\Delta G$, the right-hand side equals 1, so the crossing point of the forward work distribution and the mirrored reverse distribution is the free energy difference.

其中 $P_F(W)$ 是 A 到 B 的做功分布，$P_R(-W)$ 是 B 到 A 做功分布取镜像后的分布。一个直观结论是：当 $W=\Delta G$ 时，等式右边为 1，所以正向做功分布和反向镜像分布的交点就是自由能差。

In practice we usually don't literally find the crossing by eye; instead we use the Bennett Acceptance Ratio (BAR) or maximum likelihood estimation to combine the two work distributions and estimate $\Delta G$. This is why NEQ is often described as the Jarzynski-Crooks-BAR route.

实际计算里通常不会真的只用肉眼找交点，而是使用 Bennett Acceptance Ratio (BAR) 或最大似然估计，把两组做功分布合并起来估计 $\Delta G$。这也是为什么 NEQ 经常被描述为 Jarzynski-Crooks-BAR 路线。

## What the "short pull" is actually pulling / "短拉伸"到底在拉什么

The "pull" in NEQ is easy to misunderstand. It is not steered MD physically pulling the ligand out of the pocket, nor adding a spring between two atoms.

NEQ 里的"拉伸"容易让人误会。它不是 Steered MD 里把配体从口袋里物理拉出来，也不是给某两个原子加弹簧。

What is being pulled here is the **λ knob in chemical space**.

这里拉的是**化学空间里的 λ 旋钮**。

For example, suppose we compare two ligands:

比如我们要比较两个配体：

- A: `-H` on the benzene ring / 苯环上是 `-H`
- B: `-CH3` on the benzene ring / 苯环上是 `-CH3`

In an NEQ short pull, the simulation starts from an equilibrium configuration of A; then over the next 50 to 200 ps the force-field parameters are changed continuously: the interactions corresponding to `-H` gradually disappear, while the van der Waals radius, charges, bond angles, and dihedrals corresponding to `-CH3` gradually appear. Meanwhile the surrounding water, protein side chains, and the ligand itself keep moving according to Newton's equations.

在 NEQ 短拉伸中，模拟从 A 的平衡构象开始，接下来 50 到 200 ps 内，力场参数被连续改变：原来对应 `-H` 的相互作用逐渐消失，`-CH3` 对应的范德华半径、电荷、键角、二面角等相互作用逐渐出现。与此同时，周围的水、蛋白侧链、配体自身都按照牛顿方程继续运动。

Here is a crude but useful analogy: FEP is like slowly inflating a balloon, waiting for everyone in the room to steady themselves after each small puff; NEQ is like inflating the balloon quickly in a very short time — the people around have no time to step aside gracefully, so the system produces collisions, relaxation, and dissipated work. NEQ does not try to avoid these non-equilibrium effects; it records them as a work distribution and then uses Crooks/BAR to statistically extract the equilibrium free energy.

如果用一个粗糙但有用的比喻：FEP 像是慢慢给气球打气，每打一小口气就等房间里的人重新站稳；NEQ 像是在很短时间内快速把气球打大，周围的人来不及优雅让位，于是系统产生碰撞、弛豫和耗散功。NEQ 不试图避免这些非平衡效应，而是把它们记录成做功分布，再用 Crooks/BAR 从统计上把平衡态自由能提取出来。

The short pull can be neither too short nor too long:

短拉伸不能太短，也不能太长：

- Too short (e.g. 1 ps): the system has almost no time to respond, the dissipated work is huge, and the forward/reverse distributions may separate completely. / 如果太短，例如 1 ps，体系几乎没有时间响应，耗散功巨大，正反向分布可能完全分离。
- Too long (e.g. 10 ns): the process approaches quasi-static and statistical noise drops, but each trajectory becomes expensive and NEQ's high-concurrency advantage is eaten up. / 如果太长，例如 10 ns，过程接近准静态，统计噪音会下降，但每条轨迹太贵，NEQ 的高并发优势就被吃掉了。
- Empirically, many protein–ligand RBFE cases try 50 to 200 ps non-equilibrium transitions, then judge usability by whether the forward and reverse work distributions overlap. / 经验上，很多蛋白-配体 RBFE 场景会尝试 50 到 200 ps 的非平衡转变，然后用正反向做功分布是否重叠来判断是否可用。

## A standard NEQ RBFE workflow / 一个标准 NEQ RBFE 工作流

Taking protein–ligand relative binding free energy as an example, a full NEQ calculation still needs both the water-phase and complex-phase alchemical legs. The final target is:

以蛋白-配体相对结合自由能为例，NEQ 的完整计算仍然需要水相和复合物相两套炼金路径。最终目标是：

$$
\Delta\Delta G
=
\Delta G_\text{complex}
-
\Delta G_\text{water}
$$

A common workflow looks like this.

一个常见工作流如下。

**1. End-state equilibrium sampling / 端态平衡采样**

Run end-state MD for ligand A and ligand B separately, in both the water phase and the protein–ligand complex phase. Whether NEQ is stable depends largely on whether this step captured the important end-state conformations.

分别对配体 A 和配体 B 做端态 MD，且水相和蛋白-配体复合物相都要做。NEQ 能不能稳定，很大程度上取决于这一步有没有把端态的重要构象采出来。

**2. Extract frames from the end-state trajectories / 从端态轨迹抽帧**

From A's equilibrium trajectory, extract tens to hundreds of configurations as starting points for A→B; from B's equilibrium trajectory, extract the same number of configurations as starting points for B→A.

从 A 的平衡轨迹中抽取几十到几百个构象，作为 A 到 B 的初始点；从 B 的平衡轨迹中抽取同样数量的构象，作为 B 到 A 的初始点。

**3. Batch non-equilibrium transitions / 批量非平衡转变**

For each starting configuration, launch a short trajectory that pushes $\lambda$ from 0 to 1 (or from 1 back to 0) over 50 to 200 ps. Each trajectory records the total work $W$. These short tasks barely communicate with each other and can be naturally distributed across many GPUs or cluster nodes.

对每个初始构象启动一条短轨迹，在 50 到 200 ps 内把 $\lambda$ 从 0 推到 1，或从 1 推回 0。每条轨迹记录总做功 $W$。这些短任务之间几乎没有通信，可以非常自然地分发到大量 GPU 或集群节点上。

**4. Work-distribution analysis / 做功分布分析**

Obtain the forward/reverse work distributions for the water phase and the complex phase separately, use BAR or maximum likelihood to get $\Delta G_\text{water}$ and $\Delta G_\text{complex}$, then subtract to get $\Delta\Delta G$.

分别得到水相和复合物相的正向/反向做功分布，用 BAR 或最大似然估计得到 $\Delta G_\text{water}$ 和 $\Delta G_\text{complex}$，最后相减得到 $\Delta\Delta G$。

**5. Quality control / 质量控制**

One advantage of NEQ is that QC is very intuitive: plot the forward work distribution and the mirrored reverse distribution. If the two barely overlap, the result should not be trusted. In that case, either lengthen the pulling time, increase end-state sampling, or introduce intermediate states to break one overly large change into several segments.

NEQ 的一个优点是 QC 很直观：画出正向做功分布和反向镜像分布。如果两者几乎没有重叠，结果就不应该被相信。此时要么延长拉伸时间，要么增加端态采样，要么引入中间态，把一个太大的变化拆成几段。

## The compute difference between FEP and NEQ / FEP 和 NEQ 的算力差异

FEP can of course be parallelized. In production, splitting 16 λ windows across 16 GPUs to run simultaneously is very common. Add HREX, and different windows also periodically exchange configurations.

FEP 当然可以并行。生产环境里，16 个 λ 窗口分给 16 张 GPU 同时跑是非常常见的做法。如果再加 HREX，不同窗口之间还会周期性交换构象。

So "FEP is serial" is not accurate. A more accurate statement is: **FEP's parallelism is tightly coupled; NEQ's parallelism is loosely coupled.**

所以"FEP 是串行的"这句话并不准确。更准确的说法是：**FEP 的并行是强耦合并行，NEQ 的并行是松耦合并行。**

Although FEP windows can run at the same time, they often need:

FEP 的窗口之间虽然可以同时跑，但它们往往需要：

- Similar run speeds; / 相似的运行速度；
- Stable, low-latency communication; / 稳定的低延迟通信；
- Synchronously advancing replica exchange; / 同步推进的 replica exchange；
- A reliable network result only after the whole set of windows has converged. / 一整套窗口都收敛后才能给出可靠网络结果。

NEQ's short trajectories are more like a pile of independent small tasks. One A→B trajectory finishes on one GPU while another B→A trajectory finishes on another machine, with no need to wait for each other. As long as all the work values can be collected at the end, you can move on to analysis.

NEQ 的短轨迹则更像一堆独立小任务。某条 A 到 B 轨迹在一张 GPU 上跑完，另一条 B 到 A 轨迹在另一台机器上跑完，中间不需要相互等待。只要最后能收集所有 work values，就可以进入分析。

This brings a very practical difference: if you have a batch of fragmented GPU resources, NEQ can more easily saturate them; if you have a stable, low-latency HPC environment suited to HREX, a mature FEP pipeline is still very strong.

这带来一个很实际的差别：如果你有一批碎片化 GPU 资源，NEQ 更容易把它们吃满；如果你有一套稳定、通信快、适合 HREX 的 HPC 环境，成熟 FEP 流程仍然非常强。

## Accuracy: the two fail in different ways / 准确性：两者失败的方式不同

Neither FEP nor NEQ is magic. Their error sources differ.

FEP 和 NEQ 都不是魔法。它们的误差来源不同。

FEP's biggest fear is that intermediate windows never truly equilibrate. For example, a ligand mutation may require a protein loop or a key side chain to rearrange, but some λ windows get stuck in a local minimum and never cross the barrier within the limited simulation time. This introduces systematic error when stitching together the $\Delta G$ of neighboring windows.

FEP 最怕的是中间窗口没有真正平衡。比如配体突变导致蛋白 loop 或关键侧链需要重排，但某些 λ 窗口卡在局部极小值里，有限模拟时间内没有跨过能垒。这会让相邻窗口的 $\Delta G$ 拼接出现系统误差。

NEQ's biggest fear is dissipated work being too large. If A and B differ too much, a short pull creates severe atomic clashes or strong charge rearrangement, the forward/reverse work distributions separate completely, and BAR has no sufficient overlap region to use. Then, even with many trajectories, you have only measured an unusable non-equilibrium process more precisely.

NEQ 最怕的是耗散功太大。如果 A 和 B 差异过大，短拉伸会制造严重原子碰撞或强烈电荷重排，正反向做功分布完全分开，BAR 没有足够重叠区域可用。此时即使轨迹数很多，也只是更精确地测量了一个不可用的非平衡过程。

A practical decision table can be written like this:

一个实用决策表可以这样写：

| Scenario / 场景 | Recommended / 更推荐 | Reason / 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| Small R-group tweaks on the same scaffold / 同一母核的小 R 基团微调 | FEP / FEP+ | Mature engineering, good force-field and network-analysis ecosystem / 工程成熟，力场和网络分析生态好 |
| Molecular glue or PROTAC ternary complex, with local flexibility at the interface / 分子胶或 PROTAC 三元复合物，界面有局部柔性 | NEQ worth trying / NEQ 可重点尝试 | After end-state sampling, short trajectories explore more easily at high throughput / 端态构象采样后，短轨迹更容易高通量探索 |
| Large charge change, drastic atom creation/annihilation / 电荷变化很大、原子生成/湮灭剧烈 | FEP is more stable / FEP 更稳 | Many windows + soft-core dilute the high-energy process more smoothly / 多窗口和 soft-core 可以更平滑地稀释高能过程 |
| Need to quickly scan many candidate mutations, with fragmented GPU resources / 需要快速扫大量候选突变，且 GPU 资源碎片化 | NEQ | Many short tasks are naturally suited to high-concurrency scheduling / 大量短任务天然适合高并发调度 |
| Two ligands with very different scaffolds / 两个配体骨架差异很大 | Both need caution / 都要谨慎 | May need path splitting, intermediate states, or redefined mapping / 可能需要拆路径、中间态或重新定义 mapping |

## FEP and FEP+ are not the same thing / FEP 和 FEP+ 不是一回事

FEP is a method; FEP+ is a product.

FEP 是方法，FEP+ 是产品。

More specifically, FEP+ is Schrödinger's commercialized, end-to-end pipeline built around FEP/BAR/HREX/REST and related techniques. Its real strength is not just "being able to compute FEP," but engineering away the steps in free energy calculation that are most error-prone and most expert-time-consuming.

更具体地说，FEP+ 是 Schrödinger 围绕 FEP/BAR/HREX/REST 等技术做出的商业化完整管线。它真正强的地方不只是"会算 FEP"，而是把自由能计算里最容易出错、最耗专家时间的环节工程化了。

| Dimension / 维度 | Self-built FEP pipeline / 自建 FEP 流程 | Schrödinger FEP+ |
|---|---|---|
| Force field / 力场 | GAFF, CGenFF, OpenFF, etc.; user must judge parameter quality / GAFF、CGenFF、OpenFF 等，需要用户判断参数质量 | Deep OPLS integration, highly engineered parameter system / OPLS 系列深度集成，参数体系高度工程化 |
| Topology mapping / 拓扑映射 | Relies on pmx, LOMAP, manual checking and lots of tuning / 依赖 pmx、LOMAP、手工检查和大量调参 | FEP Mapper auto-plans the perturbation network / FEP Mapper 自动规划微扰网络 |
| Sampling / 采样 | Standard MD, HREX; enhanced sampling must be designed yourself / 常规 MD、HREX，增强采样需自行设计 | HREX + REST and similar capabilities highly integrated / HREX + REST 等能力集成度高 |
| Error diagnosis / 误差诊断 | Must build cycle closure, reweighting, and network checks yourself / 需要自己做闭环、重加权和网络检查 | Automatic cycle closure and network-level error analysis / 自动 cycle closure 和网络级误差分析 |
| Target users / 适用人群 | People with comp-chem experience willing to maintain a pipeline / 有计算化学经验、愿意维护管线的人 | Industrial R&D teams needing stable SAR predictions / 需要稳定 SAR 预测的工业研发团队 |

So discussing "FEP vs FEP+" is essentially comparing "a statistical-mechanics method" with "an industrial-grade software system built around that method." It is like comparing the principle of an engine with a car that can already drive on the road: the former explains why it can move, the latter solves how to get to the destination stably, quickly, and with few pitfalls.

所以讨论"FEP vs FEP+"时，本质上是在比较"统计力学方法"与"围绕该方法构建的工业级软件系统"。这就像比较发动机原理和一台已经能上路的车：前者解释为什么能动，后者解决怎么稳定、快速、少踩坑地到达目的地。

## What platform options exist for NEQ today / NEQ 目前有哪些平台选择

The NEQ ecosystem does not yet have a single, dominant, one-click commercial black box like FEP+. More common are academic open-source tools and self-built workflows.

NEQ 生态还没有出现一个像 FEP+ 那样占据绝对主导地位的一键式商业黑盒。更常见的是学术开源工具和自建工作流。

**GROMACS + pmx**

This is one of the most classic and mature NEQ RBFE routes. pmx can generate hybrid topologies and, together with GROMACS, run end-state sampling and non-equilibrium transitions, providing work-value analysis, BAR estimation, and distribution plots. For anyone already familiar with GROMACS, this is the first route worth trying.

这是目前最经典、最成熟的 NEQ RBFE 路线之一。pmx 可以生成 hybrid topology，配合 GROMACS 做端态采样和非平衡转变，并提供 work value 分析、BAR 估计和分布图。对于已经熟悉 GROMACS 的人，这是最值得优先尝试的路线。

**OpenMM + perses / custom NCMC workflows**

OpenMM's advantage is its Python API and programmability. perses and tools in the Chodera lab ecosystem lean toward composable relative free energy and NCMC frameworks. If your goal is to hook free energy calculation into your own backend service, virtual screening platform, or automated scheduling system, the OpenMM route is more flexible.

OpenMM 的优势是 Python API 和可编程性。perses 以及 Chodera lab 生态里的工具更偏向可组合的相对自由能与 NCMC 框架。如果目标是把自由能计算接到自己的后端服务、虚拟筛选平台或自动化调度系统里，OpenMM 路线会更灵活。

**BioExcel Building Blocks (BioBB)**

BioBB is more like workflow glue. The difficulty of NEQ is not just how to run a single trajectory, but how to generate, schedule, collect, and analyze hundreds of short trajectories. BioBB is good for stringing together GROMACS, pmx, Snakemake, or other workflow managers, reducing the mess of temporary files and script management.

BioBB 更像工作流胶水。NEQ 的难点不只是单条轨迹怎么跑，而是几百条短轨迹如何生成、调度、收集、分析。BioBB 适合把 GROMACS、pmx、Snakemake 或其他工作流管理器串起来，减少临时文件和脚本管理的混乱。

**OpenEye / NCMC-related commercial solutions**

OpenEye is not a typical "pure NEQ RBFE" platform, but Nonequilibrium Candidate Monte Carlo (NCMC) and related sampling methods are valuable for handling water networks, pocket rearrangement, and local conformational jumps. If budget and licensing allow, you can view it as a class of solutions in the commercial ecosystem adjacent to the non-equilibrium sampling idea.

OpenEye 不是典型的"纯 NEQ RBFE"平台，但 Nonequilibrium Candidate Monte Carlo (NCMC) 和相关采样方法在处理水网络、口袋重排、局部构象跳跃时很有价值。如果预算和许可证允许，可以把它看作商业生态里与非平衡采样思想相邻的一类方案。

## Final decision criteria / 最后的判断标准

I would treat FEP and NEQ as complementary tools, not one replacing the other.

我会把 FEP 和 NEQ 看作互补工具，而不是谁替代谁。

FEP's strengths are maturity, robustness, and a complete ecosystem — especially industrialized pipelines like FEP+ that have polished many engineering details. It suits standard SAR, small-step mutations, and projects that need an auditable closed-cycle network.

FEP 的优势是成熟、稳健、生态完整，尤其是 FEP+ 这样的工业化管线已经把很多工程细节打磨得很好。它适合标准 SAR、小步突变和需要可审计闭环网络的项目。

NEQ's strengths are fine-grained parallelism and intuitive quality control, and it has real potential in systems where the end-state conformations are clear but intermediate states easily get stuck. It suits high-throughput, end-state-driven scenarios where you need to quickly probe a batch of localized chemical changes.

NEQ 的优势是并行粒度细、质量控制直观，并且在端态构象明确但中间态容易卡住的体系里很有潜力。它适合高通量、端态驱动、需要快速试探一批局部化学变化的场景。

The genuinely practical strategy is not to pick a side first, but to ask three questions first:

真正实用的策略不是先站队，而是先问三个问题：

1. Is the chemical change from A to B small enough, and is the mapping reliable? / A 和 B 的化学变化是否足够小，mapping 是否可靠？
2. Are the end-state conformations already well sampled, especially protein loops, the water network, and key side chains? / 端态构象是否已经采样充分，尤其是蛋白 loop、水网络和关键侧链？
3. Do the forward and reverse work distributions overlap enough? / 正反向 work distributions 是否有足够重叠？

If these three questions are answered clearly, both FEP and NEQ can be reliable free energy tools. If they are not answered clearly, swapping in a fancier software name will not save the result.

如果这三个问题回答得清楚，FEP 和 NEQ 都可以成为可靠的自由能工具。如果回答不清楚，换一个更高级的软件名字也救不了结果。
